1. Publisher and contact

This policy applies to the Android application BHole (com.photino7.bhole), published by Florian Palacin.

For privacy questions or requests to exercise your rights, contact flo811@msn.com.

2. Local data and processing

BHole provides calculations and visualizations related to black holes. Scientific parameters entered for calculations are processed on the device. Display preferences, including the selected theme and the local purchase status, are stored on the device using Android DataStore. Language preferences may be managed by Android.

As described here, BHole does not require a user account or personal information to perform calculations.

3. In-app purchases via Google Play

Optional purchases that unlock paid themes use Google Play Billing. Google processes payments under its own terms and privacy policy. BHole receives technical purchase information (including the product identifier, purchase token, signature data and purchase status) to verify the entitlement and acknowledge the purchase with Google Play. The purchase status is cached locally to remember access to paid themes.

BHole does not receive payment card numbers used for the transaction. Google may retain transaction information according to its own legal obligations and terms.

Google Privacy Policy.

4. Email support

If you choose to contact the developer through BHole, your usual email application is opened. Sending a message is voluntary. If you send an email, the developer may receive your email address and any information included in the message to respond to your request. Your email provider also processes messages under its own terms.

Support conversations are retained only as long as needed to handle the request; support messages are kept for up to 12 months after the last exchange, unless legal retention is required or earlier deletion is requested.

5. Hosting of this page

This policy is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages for security purposes. This processing is governed by GitHub's practices and is separate from BHole's local preference storage.

GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Retention, security and deletion

Local preferences are stored within the application's storage. You can erase them by clearing BHole's application data in Android settings or uninstalling the app, subject to any backups and restorations managed by Android. Uninstalling BHole does not delete transaction records retained by Google Play.

BHole has no separate user account system. Purchases are processed through Google Play infrastructure; local data benefits from Android's application storage and isolation protections.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data received by the developer through support communications, and object to certain processing. Contact flo811@msn.com. You may also contact the competent data protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

8. Updates to this policy

This policy may be revised if BHole's features, integrated libraries, or data processing practices change. The latest update date is displayed at the top of the page.
